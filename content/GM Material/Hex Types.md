> "An awareness of your surroundings makes you feel like you're in a place."

Local Area Maps: 1 hex = 3 miles = 1 league
Good systems avoid fractions (half a day from here, 3/4 hex, this hex takes 4 days)
- Want smallest measurement of time to be 1 hour
- Want smallest measurement of distance to be 1 hex

- Roads: 1 Hour
- Plains or Hills: 2 Hours
- Bog or Forest: 4 Hours
- Mountains: 8 Hours

This creates the feeling of progress and meaningful decisions.

3 miles away, you can effectively see what lies in the next hex.

You stumble upon a wooden fort, you clear it out, and get a rough map that states there's another dungeon in the nearby bog! You don't know exactly where it's at, but now you can go find it.
