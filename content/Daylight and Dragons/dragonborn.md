# dragonborn

[[dragonborn]] are a race of legend in the campaign setting.

## Known Details

- The masked attackers from Session 1 were discovered to be dragonborn.
- In Session 2, [[Rowan I]] disguised himself as a dragonborn at [[The Cast Iron]].
- [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]] treated the disguised Rowan as someone connected to an expected task.
