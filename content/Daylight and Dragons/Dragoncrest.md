# Dragoncrest

[[Dragoncrest]] is the starting city for the campaign.

## Known Details

- Population: about 10,000.
- The city has a wall.
- The city contains a forest within its walls.
- The manicured surface section of the forest is maintained and monitored; visitors are recorded and registered.
- A deeper, less controlled section of woods exists inside the city.
- These are the only woods known in the immediate region.
- The settlement relies significantly on the orchard and manicured forest areas.

## Districts and Places

- [[The Mound]]
- [[The Cast Iron]]
- Wealthy district: gold is filigreed and carved; plants are displayed as signs of wealth.
