# The Cast Iron Job

[[The Cast Iron Job]] is the broader thread around the work [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]] expected disguised [[Rowan I]] to complete.

## Known Details

- Rowan entered [[The Cast Iron]] disguised as a [[dragonborn]].
- The blacksmith treated Rowan as connected to an unfinished task.
- The blacksmith offered half payment now and half after proof.
- The 2026-08-22 continuation clarified the immediate proof as [[Proof of Nelly's Demise]].
- [[Cast Iron Elf]] later came from [[The Cast Iron]] to [[Lio's House]] and disappeared with [[Mark]].

## Open Questions

- Who is ultimately paying for the job?
- What proof will satisfy the blacksmith?
- Is [[Cast Iron Elf]] enforcing the job, recovering someone, or acting on a different order?
- Is the job connected to [[Drunken Soberbeard]]?
