# Nelly's Home

[[Nelly's Home]] is a tiny residence in the [[Undercity]], carved into the interior side of [[Dragoncrest]]'s outer wall.

## Known Details

- The entrance is a tiny hole.
- Everyone in the party was able to fit or crawl inside.
- It contains a small sleeping nest, a small dresser, shared restrooms, and a tiny table.
- A rope trap triggered around [[Mark]]'s throat, dealing 15 force damage before [[Mattalic Longlisten]] cut him free.
- [[Nelly]] said the trap was not hers.

## Status

- Compromised. The party believes Nelly's enemies may know where she lives.
