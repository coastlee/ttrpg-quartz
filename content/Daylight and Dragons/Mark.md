# Mark

[[Mark]] is an orc barbarian played by Erick.

## Session Notes

- In Session 2, Mark took the satchel of coins from [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]].
- Mark noticed the coin bag getting lighter as the party moved away from [[The Cast Iron]].
- Mark failed a Dexterity save, bumped into [[Leo]], and spilled the coins, revealing the bag was empty.
- In the street ambush, Mark took 6 damage, landed greataxe hits, and threw a handaxe at the remaining survivor.
