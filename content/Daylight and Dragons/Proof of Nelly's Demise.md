# Proof of Nelly's Demise

[[Proof of Nelly's Demise]] is the task the party was sent to complete after deceiving [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]].

## Known Details

- The party convinced the blacksmith they were [[dragonborn]].
- The blacksmith sent them to reclaim proof of [[Nelly]]'s demise.
- Nelly remains worried about what proof the party will provide.
- One option discussed was taking Nelly prisoner and having [[Rowan I]] disguise himself as her.

## Open Questions

- What proof will satisfy [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]]?
- Can the party fake proof without harming Nelly?
- Does [[Cast Iron Elf]] change the deadline or stakes?
