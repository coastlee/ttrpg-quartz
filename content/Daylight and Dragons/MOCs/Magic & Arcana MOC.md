# Magic & Arcana MOC

> Up: [[Daylight and Dragons/Daylights and Dragons Home|Daylight and Dragons Home]]

Use this MOC to track spells, magical items, arcane laws, curses, rituals, magical creatures, and unexplained phenomena.

## Setting Magic

- Magic is common.
- Weapons are rare.
- A [[Magic Coin Bag]] with runes emptied itself after the party left [[The Cast Iron]].
- The Magic Coin Bag appeared bottomless during the 2026-08-22 session, and a dropped stone disappeared into it.
