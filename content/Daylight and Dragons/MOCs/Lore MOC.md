# Lore MOC

> Up: [[Daylight and Dragons/Daylights and Dragons Home|Daylight and Dragons Home]]

Use this MOC to track revealed setting facts, history, legends, cosmology, and recurring mysteries.

## Revealed Setting Facts

- The world is made out of precious metals.
- Most people are nocturnal.
- People who go out during the daytime disappear.
- Wood, dirt, plants, trees, and woodland meats are signs of wealth.
- Wealthy people have engraved and filigreed metal, while poor gold is often lumpy and poorly shaped.
- Magic is common.
- Weapons are rare.
- [[Dragoncrest]] contains the only known woods in the immediate region.

## Legends and Myths

- [[dragonborn]] are a race of legend.

## Local Lore

- [[Dragoncrest]] relies on the production of its orchard and manicured forest areas.
- Visitors to the maintained surface section of the forest are recorded and registered.
- [[The Mound]] is built entirely out of gold.
- [[The Cast Iron]] uses wood in its forging process.
- [[Undercity]] folk know [[Drunken Soberbeard]] as a dangerous debt collector and crime boss.
- Drunken Soberbeard is accused of helping missing persons disappear.

## Lore Questions

- Why do people disappear during the daytime?
- Why is the world made primarily of precious metals?
- Why is wood so valuable?
- What is the true status of [[dragonborn]] in the world?
- Are Drunken Soberbeard's missing-person rumors related to the setting's daytime disappearances?
