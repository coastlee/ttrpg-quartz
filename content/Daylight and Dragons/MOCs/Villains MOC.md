# Villains MOC

Use this MOC to track confirmed villains, antagonists, hostile forces, and suspect masterminds.

## Confirmed Hostile Forces

- [[Masked Attackers]] - attacked or harassed [[Nelly]] at [[The Mound]].
- [[Street Pursuers]] - followed and fought the party after they left [[The Cast Iron]].
- [[Cast Iron Elf]] - came from [[The Cast Iron]] to [[Lio's House]] and disappeared with [[Mark]].

## Suspects and Possible Antagonists

- [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]] - expects proof from the disguised [[Rowan I]] and may be connected to the attack on [[Nelly]].
- [[Drunken Soberbeard]] - dwarf crime boss tied to Nelly's debt and accused of helping missing persons disappear.
- [[Hooded Blackguard]] - watcher seen after the street ambush and from [[The Mound]].
- [[Werewolf Referral Person]] - may have been planted to direct Nelly to Drunken Soberbeard.
- Unknown employer behind [[The Cast Iron Job]].
- Unknown force behind daytime disappearances.
