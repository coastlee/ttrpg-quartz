# Relationships MOC

Use this MOC to track alliances, debts, obligations, grudges, loyalties, and suspicions.

## Party Relationships

- Party and [[Nelly]]: the party defended Nelly from the [[Masked Attackers]] and is investigating why Nelly was targeted.
- Party and [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]]: the party is deceiving the blacksmith through [[Rowan I]]'s dragonborn disguise and owes proof tomorrow.
- Party and [[Mattalic Longlisten]]: Longlisten joined the party after his trade at [[The Cast Iron]] and agreed to guide them through the woods.
- Party and [[Street Pursuers]]: hostile after the street ambush.
- Party and [[Drunken Soberbeard]]: indirect conflict after learning of Nelly's debt and missing son.
- Party and [[Cast Iron Elf]]: hostile after the elf disappeared with [[Mark]].
