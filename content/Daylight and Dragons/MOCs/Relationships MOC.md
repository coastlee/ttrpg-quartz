# Relationships MOC

Use this MOC to track alliances, debts, obligations, grudges, loyalties, and suspicions.

## Party Relationships

- Party and [[Nelly]]: the party defended Nelly from the [[Masked Attackers]] and is investigating why Nelly was targeted.
- Party and [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]]: the party is deceiving the blacksmith through [[Rowan I]]'s dragonborn disguise and owes proof tomorrow.
- Party and [[Mattalic Longlisten]]: Longlisten joined the party after his trade at [[The Cast Iron]] and agreed to guide them through the woods.
- Party and [[Street Pursuers]]: hostile after the street ambush.

## Character-Specific Relationships

- [[Rowan I]] and [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]]: Rowan successfully passed as a [[dragonborn]] and became the focus of the blacksmith's expected job.
- [[Mark]] and [[Magic Coin Bag]]: Mark took the bag, noticed it getting lighter, and spilled the coins when the bag proved empty.
- [[Leo]] and [[Street Pursuers]]: Leo contacted one follower with Message before combat and targeted the oaf during the fight.
- [[Mattalic Longlisten]] and [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]]: the blacksmith offered Longlisten a blanket and rope to guide the party through the woods.

## Suspected Relationships

- [[The Cast Iron]] may be connected to the [[Masked Attackers]].
- [[Street Pursuers]] may have been following the party because of the [[Magic Coin Bag]].
- The [[Dragonborn Masks]] may be useful as proof for [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]].

## Maintenance
