# Cultures MOC

Use this MOC to track customs, class markers, social norms, languages, taboos, and material culture.

## Cultural Norms

- People are nocturnal.
- Going out during the daytime is dangerous and associated with disappearances.
- Wood, dirt, plants, trees, and woodland creatures are signs of wealth.
- Wealthy gold is engraved, carved, and filigreed.
- Poor gold is often lumpy and poorly shaped.
- Lavish dishes may include meats from woodland creatures.

## Dragoncrest Culture

- The wealthy district displays plants openly as status symbols.
- Visitors to the maintained forest section are recorded and registered.
- Access to wood appears economically and socially significant.
