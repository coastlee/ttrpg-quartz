# Cities & Settlements MOC

> Up: [[Daylight and Dragons/Daylights and Dragons Home|Daylight and Dragons Home]]

Use this MOC to track towns, cities, neighborhoods, political centers, and local services.

## Cities

- [[Dragoncrest]] - starting city, population about 10,000

## Dragoncrest Districts and Sites

- [[The Mound]] - starting pub or inn
- [[The Cast Iron]] - wealthy-district blacksmith
- [[Undercity]] - lower district with more crime and desperate activity
- [[Nelly's Home]] - compromised home carved into the interior side of Dragoncrest's outer wall
- [[Lio's House]] - dormitory, group home, or homeless shelter in the Undercity
- Wealthy district - marked by filigreed gold, carved metal, visible plants, and wood access
- Walled forest and orchard areas - registered and controlled surface section, deeper untamed woods

## Services and Institutions

- Blacksmithing: [[The Cast Iron]]
- Hospitality: [[The Mound]]
- Shelter or group home: [[Lio's House]]
- Guilds: [[Chef's Guild]]
- Forest/orchard access: authority unknown

## Settlement Questions

- Who governs [[Dragoncrest]]?
- Who records and registers visitors to the woods?
- Who controls the orchard and forest production?
- How does nocturnal life shape city services and law?
- Who controls or polices the [[Undercity]]?

## Maintenance

- Add districts as separate notes when the party revisits them.
- Track civic authorities, laws, major employers, gates, markets, temples, and hazards.
