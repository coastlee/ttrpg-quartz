# Open Questions MOC

Use this MOC to keep mysteries and unanswered campaign facts visible between sessions.

## Active Questions

- Who sent the masked figures to harass [[Nelly]]?
- Why was [[Nelly]] targeted?
- Why were the attackers wearing masks?
- Were the attackers truly [[dragonborn]], or was something else making them appear that way?
- How common is knowledge of [[dragonborn]] in [[Dragoncrest]]?
- Are the [[Dragonborn Masks]] useful as proof for [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]]?

### The Cast Iron

- What task does the blacksmith believe disguised [[Rowan I]] is completing?
- What proof does [[The Cast Iron]] expect tomorrow?
- Who is ultimately paying for the job?
- Is [[The Cast Iron]] acting independently or on behalf of a faction?
- Is [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]] connected to the [[Masked Attackers]]?

### Magic Coin Bag

- What do the runes inside the bag do?
- Did the coins return to [[The Cast Iron]]?
- Can the bag be used to track or identify the party?
- Was the emptying bag a trap, a test, or normal payment procedure?
- Were the [[Street Pursuers]] following the party because of the [[Magic Coin Bag]]?

### Setting Mysteries

- What causes people to disappear during the daytime?
- Why is the world made primarily of precious metals?
- Why are plants, dirt, wood, and woodland creatures signs of wealth?
- Who controls access to forests and orchards?
- Why are weapons rare when magic is common?
