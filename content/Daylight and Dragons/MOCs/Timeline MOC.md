Use this MOC to track campaign chronology, world history revealed at the table, and deadlines.

## Campaign Timeline

### Before Play

- The world is made out of precious metals.
- Most people are nocturnal.
- People who go out during the daytime disappear.
- Wood, dirt, plants, trees, and woodland meats are signs of wealth.
- Magic is common and weapons are rare.
- [[Dragoncrest]] contains the only known woods in the immediate region.

### Session 1

- Source: [[Daylight and Dragons/parsed_notes/2026-06-27|2026-06-27 Game Log]]
- The campaign opened at [[The Mound]], a pub owned by [[Nelly]] in [[Dragoncrest]].
- Masked figures harassed [[Nelly]].
- The party fought and killed the attackers.
- The party took the attackers' masks and discovered the attackers were [[dragonborn]], a race of legend.

### Session 2

- Source: [[Daylight and Dragons/parsed_notes/2026-06-27|2026-06-27 Game Log]]
- The party investigated why someone might target [[Nelly]].
- The party went to the wealthy district of [[Dragoncrest]].
- The party visited [[The Cast Iron]], a blacksmith that uses wood in its forging process.
- [[Rowan I]] used magic to disguise himself as a dragonborn.
- The blacksmith treated disguised Rowan as connected to an unfinished task.
- The blacksmith gave the party a coin bag and asked for proof tomorrow.
- [[Mattalic Longlisten]] agreed to guide the party through the woods in exchange for a blanket and rope.
- The coin bag emptied itself after the party left [[The Cast Iron]].
- The party noticed they were being followed and fought the pursuers.

### Session 3

- Source: [[Daylight and Dragons/parsed_notes/2026-08-22|2026-08-22 Game Log]]
- After the street ambush, the party found the [[Magic Coin Bag]] appeared bottomless and could make a stone disappear.
- The party returned to [[The Mound]] and told [[Nelly]] that [[The Cast Iron]] wanted proof of her demise.
- Nelly fled, triggering a chase through the [[Undercity]].
- A trap at [[Nelly's Home]] nearly strangled [[Mark]], dealing 15 force damage before [[Mattalic Longlisten]] cut him free.
- Nelly revealed debt to [[Drunken Soberbeard]] and that [[Nelly's Son]] was taken about three months earlier after she missed payments.
- Nelly described [[Werewolf Referral Person]], who referred her to Drunken Soberbeard.
- At [[Lio's House]], [[Cast Iron Elf]] arrived from [[The Cast Iron]] and disappeared with [[Mark]].
