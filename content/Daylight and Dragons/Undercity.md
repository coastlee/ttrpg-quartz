# Undercity

The [[Undercity]] is a lower area of [[Dragoncrest]] associated with crime, desperate choices, and people with few resources.

## Known Details

- It has much more crime and other activity than the areas previously seen by the party.
- [[Nelly's Home]] is carved into the interior side of [[Dragoncrest]]'s outer wall.
- [[Lio's House]] is located here.
- [[Drunken Soberbeard]] is known among Undercity folk.
