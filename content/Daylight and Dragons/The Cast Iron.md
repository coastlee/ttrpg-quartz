# The Cast Iron

[[The Cast Iron]] is a blacksmith in the wealthy district of [[Dragoncrest]].

## Known Details

- The shop uses wood in the forging process, making it notable in a setting where wood and plants signal wealth.
- There were six or seven workers inside when the party arrived.
- The shop smelled of dirt and earth.
- [[Rowan I]] entered disguised as a [[dragonborn]], causing the shop leader to close the blacksmith to other business.
- [[The Cast Iron Blacksmith]] appeared to believe the disguised Rowan was involved in an unfinished task.

## Session 2 Events

- The blacksmith gave the party a coin bag as "half now, half when you have proof."
- The blacksmith asked the party to bring proof tomorrow for the next job.
- The coin bag later emptied itself after the party left.
- Session 3 clarified the expected proof as proof of [[Nelly]]'s demise.
- An elf from The Cast Iron later came to [[Lio's House]], said he was looking for his friend, and disappeared with [[Mark]].
