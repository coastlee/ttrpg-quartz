From the **doughguy.co**:

Dough Ball: 188g each
Total flour: 666g
Water: 413g
Hydration: 62%

## Ingredients

|          Ingredient          | Amount | Baker's % |
| :--------------------------: | :----- | --------- |
| Bread Flour (12-14% protein) | 666g   | 100%      |
|   Water (cold, under 60F)    | 413g   | 62%       |
|     Yeast (Active, Dry)      | 2.7g   | 0.4%      |
|             Salt             | 17g    | 2.5%      |
|            Sugar             | 13g    | 2%        |
|          Olive Oil           | 22g    | 3.3%      |
## Instructions

1. Cool water to under 60 degrees F
2. Mix water + active dry yeast
3. Add flour + olive oil, mix 2 minutes
4. Add sugar and salt on low
5. Mix 10 more minutes
6. Cover, Rest 1-3 hours
7. Shape into balls, seal seam
8. Refrigerate 2-4 days (3 IDEAL)
9. Bring to Room Temp before using
