Zephrax-9 is in the hex 1008 which is [[Crimson Contract]] territory.

- **Description:** A lush, jungle world teeming with life. Its dense forests are home to massive, bioluminescent flora and fauna. Settlements are sparse, with inhabitants relying on eco-domes to protect against predatory megafauna. It’s a hub for bio-research and exotic medicinal exports.
- **Inhabitants:** Felans, humans, and daring Borians seeking new trade opportunities.
