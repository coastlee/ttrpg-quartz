rules outlined by **The Perilous Void** and made it so nearly every entity had jump gates, making it possible

### Steps 14-15: Generate the Star System

We took a look at our still unnamed star system and assigned population centers. This included a populated 
planet on [[Kragos-7]], [[Veyra Prime]] is an asteroid world with a planetary colony, and we decided that
[[Tarsis IV]] has a lunar outpost.

![[20250719.SystemProfile.png]]

### Other Details

We decided that [[Zephrax-9]] was going to be in **Crimson Contract Space** on **Hex: 1008**. That means
that [[Ten]]'s contact is very, *very* far away. We will have quite the trip ahead of us!

- The reason [[Zephrax-9]] wound up being so far away came from a *mythic roll*; we gave it a *very likely*
modifier that it would have been in USF space, but mythic said no. The justifcation was given that [[Ten]]'s
contact likely would not have been in NTU space... so pirates it is!
