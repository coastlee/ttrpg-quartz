Star Wars COUSINS

Episode I Shadows of the Floating Palace

It is a time of fragile peace. Eight years after the Exar Kun Wars, the galaxy licks its wounds while new shadows rise.

On the edge of the Outer Rim, two stolen ships carry unlikely heroes toward destiny.

Aboard a battered freighter, HERA OHTANI and the fierce K’RLA “CLAW” cut through the silence of an asteroid field, guided only by the dubious memory of a gum-chewing Nautolan smuggler. Somewhere among the drifting rocks hides a secret worth stealing… or worth dying for.

Meanwhile, on the glittering yet poisoned world of CODIA, TSOCK SEFF and KOTH ATREIDES walk the streets of Alsain beneath the distant silhouette of the Maknir’ajir—the opulent sky-palace of the tyrannical Sildeeva dynasty. Slavery festers in the mines and scrapyards below, while the palace drifts above the clouds, untouchable and unseen.

Whispers of rebellion stir. A clandestine group known only as the LAC prepares to strike. In fuel stops and shadowed alleys, credits change hands, holo-IDs fall to the floor, and dangerous alliances are forged.

As the twin suns set and the floating palace casts its long shadow once more, four cousins from forgotten houses stand on the brink: drawn by blood, bound by fate, and hunted by an empire of wealth that has never known defeat.

Their choices will echo across the stars… and the galaxy will never be the same.
