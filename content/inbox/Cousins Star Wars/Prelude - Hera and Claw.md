The stolen starship came out of hyperspace, appearing suddenly in a small field of asteroids. The planet **Batuu** was visible—about marble-sized—in the distance. It was soundless and uneventful, as the space beyond atmosphere's and space stations are prone to be. The noiseless drifting of the chunks of spacerock were the only feeling of anything. If, well...
