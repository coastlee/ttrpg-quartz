**Up.** [[Online Games]]

[[Ultramarine Archipelago]] uses the **Worlds Without Number** system. Their website is listed [here](https://the-ultramarine-archipelago.tiddlyhost.com/?page=character_creation). It is a public discord server.

It's quiet here. They do a mixture of live sessions and play-by-post sessions. I've been a member since Feb. 22, 2024.

Since then, I've probably been through 5 adventures. 2 of which may have died before concluding.
