### Generic Advancement

- Roll Additional Hit Points
	- If you roll less than current, it goes up by 1
- Improved Attack Bonus
- Improve Saving Throws
	- Decrease by 1
- Gain & Spend Skill Points
	- 3 points per level
	- If expert / partial expert, get 1 bonus non-combat skill
- Add New Spells and Arts
- Purchase Improved Attributes
- Choose New Foci
