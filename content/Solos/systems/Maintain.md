# Maintain (I)

A spell to extend the life of a target spell by its original Duration. Only the caster's own spells can be extended and Maintain must be cast before the target spell expires. The source / center of the target spell must be within [[CSI]] hexes of the caster.

## Bonus Effects

**[[CML]] 80+** Another mage's spell may be maintained, provided the caster is aware of its nature and knows a reasonably similar spell.<br/>

**Time:** 25 - [[CSI]] seconds<br/>
**Range:** [[CSI]] hexes<br/>
**Duration:** As per Target Spell<br/>
