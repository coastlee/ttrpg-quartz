# Dispell (I)

A spell to remove an enchantment. *Dispell* cannot counter a spell currently being cast, only existing magic / effects.

With the basic spell, the caster must be able to cast the target enchantment (or something very similar), and only *Temporary* enchantments can be dispelled.

The success roll is modified by ten (10) times the complexity of the target spell, ward, etc. This is in addition to the normal five (5) point penalty for a first level spell. For example, to remove a third level enchantment, [[Effective Mastery Level|EML]] is `-30` for the target spell and `-5` for *Dispel* for a total of `-35`.

## Bonus Effects

**[[CML]] 60+** Unknown enchantments may be dispelled.<br/>
**[[CML]] 80+** Indefinite enchantments may be dispelled.<br/>

***Note 1.*** *Critical Failure when casting Dispell might make the target enchantment misfire.*<br/>
***Note 2.*** *A Permanent enchantment can never be dispelled.*<br/>

**Time:** 25 - [[CSI]] seconds<br/>
**Range:** CSI hexes to target enchantment<br/>
