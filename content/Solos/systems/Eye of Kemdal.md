# Eye of Kemdal (I)

A spell permitting the caster to survey the local environment for the nearest spatio-temporal discontinuity. If the nearest discontinuity is within the spell's *Range*, the caster learns the direction in which it lies and also its general type, namely a [[Barasi Point]], [[Godstone]], etc.

With [[CS]], the caster learns the distance to the discontinuity.

*Eye of Kemdal* is prone to disruption, and cannot work properly when cast within [[CSI]] hexes of a *convocational* sanctum, enchanted artifact, active spell, etc. An [[Effective Mastery Level|EML]] penalty of 10-30 should apply at GM discretion.

## Bonus Effects

**[[CML]] 70+** Caster may exclude the nearest discontinuity from the survey.<br/>

**Time:** 15 - [[CSI]] minutes<br/>
**Range:** [[CSI]] miles<br/>
**Duration:** N/A<br/>
