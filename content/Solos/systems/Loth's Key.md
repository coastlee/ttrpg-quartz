# Loth's Key (III)

A self-enchantment which allows the caster to read a document written in unknown languages / scripts. With the basic enchantment, the caster must know the script. [[Effective Mastery Level|EML]] is affected by the condition of the document (see SAVORYA 1).

The spell creates a special relationship between the caster and the one written work; this relationship cannot be extended to include other written works or readers. A separate casting is required for each written work.

Each page is presumed to take one minute to read. The caster cannot talk or write (translate) while reading.

**Special Bonus:** A caster with *[[Psychometry]]* adds that psionic talent's [[Skill Index|SI]] to [[Effective Mastery Level|EML]].

## Bonus Effects

**[[CML]] 70+** Caster may write / speak a translation of the work.<br/>
**[[CML]] 80+** Caster need not know the Script to read the document.<br/>
**[[CML]] 90+** Duration is extended to allow the caster to read the entire document.<br/>

**Time:** 15 - [[CSI]] minutes<br/>
**Range:** Touch (written work)<br/>
**Duration:** [[CSI]] minutes (pages)<br/>
