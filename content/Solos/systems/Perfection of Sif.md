# Perfection of Sif (I)

A self enchantment to increase the effectiveness of Eyesight, Hearing, or Smell. The caster selects *one* sensory attribute and then casts the spell. [[MS]] increases the chosen attribute by `1d6`, [[CS]] by `2d6`.

The [[ML]]s of skills based on the chosen attribute are raised by five times (`5x`) the attribute increase. If the skill has the attribute in its [[SB]] twice, its [[ML]] is increased by ten times (`10x`) the attribute increase. For example, if a `+4` enchantment is laid upon Smell, *[[Tracking]]* is increased by 20 and *[[Brewing]]* is increased by 40.

These sensory benefits exist for *Duration*. Each time the spell is cast (or maintained) the subject rolls `1d6` for aftereffects. Any attribute changes are permanent (cannot be dispelled).

| 1d6 | Sensory Attribute Aftereffects        |
| :-: | :------------------------------------ |
| 1-2 | Reduce chosen attribute by one (-1)   |
| 3-5 | No aftereffects                       |
|  6  | Increase chosen attribute by one (+1) |
